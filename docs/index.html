What each entry holds

The book's own words on uses, dose, botany and habitat sit beside facts gathered in October 2026. 211 of 217 entries have a photo from Wikimedia Commons, 3 from iNaturalist, 3 show a herbarium sheet from Kew, and 214 list papers from Europe PMC.

Names
The Tagalog, Visayan, Ilocano, Spanish and English names Pardo de Tavera recorded, plus the name in use today.
Description
A modern account from Wikipedia in English and, where one exists, in Tagalog.
Location
Where the plant is native, where the book says it grew, and which Philippine provinces have records of it today.
Research
The most cited recent papers with the plant in the title, linked to the publisher.

The remedies and doses are a historical record from 1901. Several of these plants are poisonous. Do not treat this site as medical advice.
